Subscriptions & Purchases

FlightFolio Pro is available through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Purchase confirmation, billing, subscription management, and refunds are handled through your Apple Account and the App Store.

  • Use Restore Purchases inside FlightFolio if an existing purchase is not recognized.
  • Manage or cancel an active subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings.
  • TestFlight purchases are sandbox transactions and do not charge your payment method.

Flight Records & Exports

If you experience a problem saving a flight, viewing progress, or exporting a record, contact support with the steps that led to the issue and, if possible, a screenshot of the problem.

Important Notice

FlightFolio is a recordkeeping and organizational tool. Pilots remain responsible for ensuring that their records and operational decisions satisfy applicable regulations, company procedures, and aviation requirements.

Typical support requests are reviewed as soon as practical.
